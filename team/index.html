Our Team

IHT's six co-founders come from healthcare, technology, business and law. Between them they bring more than 20 years with Medicaid health plans, 20 years improving access to care for underserved patients, and 25 years leading technology and consumer companies.

Keith Matsutsuyu

Co-Founder and Partner at IHT, a seasoned entrepreneur and healthcare executive dedicated to the mission of improving quality, efficiency, and communication through technology-enabled services.

Ed Meador

Chief Technology Officer and Co-Founder/Partner at IHT has over 20 years of experience working with multiple Medicaid Health Plans.

Chris Cruttenden Sr.

Co-Founder and Partner at IHT. He has worked in healthcare over the last 20 years with a focus on better access to care for the underserved populations.

Ray Mario

Co-Founder and Partner at IHT is a seasoned professional with over 25 years of experience in management roles across technology and consumer companies as well as fulfilment jobs across the country.

Anthony Wang

Co-Founder at IHT is currently a partner at O'Melveny's Newport Beach office, specializing in corporate and securities law, focusing on private equity, venture capital, M&A, and intellectual property for start-ups and investors.

Christopher Cruttenden Jr.

Chief Engineer and Co-Founder/Partner at IHT, Christopher graduated from California Polytechnic University, focusing on technology and human expression with over 5 years’ experience programming in the cryptocurrency ecosystem and blockchain.
